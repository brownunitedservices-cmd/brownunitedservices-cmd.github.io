Original Audio Drama: His Billion Dollar Betrayal

Breaking Code Studio writes, voices, and produces His Billion Dollar Betrayal, an original audio drama series blending high-stakes storytelling with faith-rooted themes of integrity, redemption, and purpose. Each episode runs approximately two minutes — designed to be binge-worthy, emotionally charged, and impossible to stop listening to.

The story follows a woman navigating power, secrets, and betrayal in a world where nothing is as it seems — crafted for listeners who want drama with depth.

About the Studio

Breaking Code Studio was founded on a simple belief: most women don't fail at creative work because they lack talent — they fail because they lack a system. The studio exists to provide that system, whether through binge-worthy audio storytelling or structured digital education.

Every drama episode is written, voiced, and produced in-house. Every digital resource is built from real workflows and tested approaches — not theory. The studio operates on a calm-authority philosophy: encouraging, structured, and faith-rooted in everything it creates.

What we make

Entertainment: Original short-form audio drama series, starting with His Billion Dollar Betrayal (10 episodes planned, 6 streaming now).
Education: Digital products, tutorials, and workshops for women building creative income with Canva.
Community: A growing audience of women who want both great stories and practical tools for financial independence.
